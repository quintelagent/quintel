Runs 24 and 64 to 78 started from the Nov 20 close ($125.53) instead of the real price, about $159 and $165. Briefings and odds published for those runs were wrong.

## What went wrong

- Run 24 was made at 9:00am ET, before the open. The token was 26% above the Nov 20 close. The sanity check rejects a gap over 25%, so the token price was ignored.
- Before runs 64 to 78, daily history had refreshed only once that day and missed the Nov 23 close. The resulting 31% gap tripped the same check.

## What we did

- History now retries every tick until the latest session is present.
- A stale close is never used when a stock quote is available.
- A large token move is accepted when the stock quote agrees with it.

## What it means for the record

- The affected runs are kept, unedited. They still verify against their stored inputs.
- The paper desk made no trades on them.
- The scorecard only uses runs made while the market is open, so it leaves them out.
