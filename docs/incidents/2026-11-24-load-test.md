A load test with 500 visitors at once found that calls to the latest-run endpoint could fail.

## What happened

- At 3:51am ET on Nov 24, 114 of 2,272 calls to `/api/latest` returned an error. Every other endpoint answered without errors.
- When the cached answer expired, every visitor's request went to the database at once, and it refused them.

## What was affected

- Only the load test, as far as we know. Runs, fingerprints and stored data were not affected.

## What was done

- The API now serves the last good copy while one request refreshes it. Live at 3:45pm ET on Nov 24.
