# Code of conduct

Be civil and stick to the work. Criticize numbers, code and ideas, not people.

Not welcome here: harassment, personal attacks, publishing anyone's private information, spam, price talk, trading advice, and asking people to connect a wallet, sign something or share a key.

Maintainers remove what breaks this and may block repeat offenders. To report something privately, use [a private advisory](https://github.com/quintelagent/quintel/security/advisories/new).
